Maison Rousseau — Our Story
37 Years in Bread

Our Story

Baked with hands,
not machines.

Maison Rousseau began in 1987 in a small village kitchen outside Lyon. Three generations later, we still follow the same unhurried process: certified organic flour sourced from six local farms, sourdough cultures fed every single day for decades, and slow overnight fermentation.

We believe bread should take its time. Our loaves proof for 16 to 48 hours depending on the season, the humidity, the weather. We start baking before dawn so that by 7am, your bread is as fresh as morning light.

Nothing is made in advance. Nothing is frozen. If we run out, we run out — and we will see you tomorrow.
